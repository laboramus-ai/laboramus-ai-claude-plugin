Laboramus AI

Your open-source job application assistant. Analyze employers, match your skills, and write tailored cover letters—all inside Claude.

Transform your job search

Laboramus AI is an open-source plugin built for Claude Cowork. It acts as your personal digital twin and career strategist, turning generic CVs into highly targeted, authentic job applications.

View on GitHub

How it works (The User Journey)

Laboramus AI makes your job search structured, analytical, and highly personalized. Here is what the process looks like:

👤

1. Build Your Profile

Simply drop your CV into the chat. Claude extracts your hard skills to build a reusable digital twin.

🔍

2. Discover Jobs

Let Claude autonomously scrape job portals or just paste a link to a role you found.

📊

3. Hard-Fact Analysis

Claude calculates your precise skill match, door-to-door commute times (SBB & Car), and spots red flags in employer reviews.

🎯

4. Tailored Application

Generate a highly personalized, authentic cover letter perfectly aligned with the role.

🤝

5. Interview Strategy

Get a customized preparation sheet and negotiation strategy for your upcoming interview.

Core Features

Intelligent Profiling

Extracts hard skills and roles directly from your CV to build a reusable digital twin.

Job Discovery

Scrapes and evaluates job postings from platforms like LinkedIn via browser automation.

Deep Employer Analysis

Researches company culture, business models, and calculates door-to-door commute times (SBB & Car).

Tailored Cover Letters

Generates authentic cover letters based on a calculated fit score between your profile and the role.

100% Local-First & Data Sovereignty

🔒 Your data stays with you!
Laboramus AI operates entirely locally on your machine. All generated data, CVs, and notes are saved exclusively in your local workspace folder. There is no cloud database and no tracking. You are in full control and decide exactly what information you share with Anthropic (Claude) or other AI providers. The responsibility for your sensitive application data lies completely with you.

Installation

You can install the plugin directly into your global Claude Code / Gemini config directory via terminal:

git clone https://github.com/laboramus-ai/laboramus-ai-claude-plugin.git ~/.gemini/config/plugins/laboramus-ai

To install via Claude Cowork Desktop App, simply add this repository URL in the Plugin Marketplace settings.
